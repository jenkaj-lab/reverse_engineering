Continues a file search from a previous call to the [FindFirstFile](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/desktop/api/fileapi/nf-fileapi-findfirstfilea), [FindFirstFileEx](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/desktop/api/fileapi/nf-fileapi-findfirstfileexa), or [FindFirstFileTransacted](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/desktop/api/winbase/nf-winbase-findfirstfiletransacteda) functions.
